Where Numbers Come Alive!

Maths, drawn so it makes sense.

Formulas and equations can feel confusing when they're just symbols on a page. MathsToons explains them with colourful motion graphics, animated characters and step-by-step working, so you can see why each step happens.

This site keeps those videos organised by class and chapter, adds a quiz at the end of each chapter, and remembers how far you've got.

How MathsToons teaches

One idea per video

Each lesson covers a single theorem, method or trick, short enough to watch between homework problems.

English + Hinglish

Lessons come in English, and many in Hinglish too, so the explanation sounds the way you'd talk it through with a friend.

See it, then prove it

Diagrams build themselves on screen, then the working follows, so the result makes sense before you have to remember it.
